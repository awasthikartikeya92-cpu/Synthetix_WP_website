— 01 / Human-in-the-Loop

Configure exactly where human judgment takes over.

Configure human review checkpoints per workflow, environment, and change classification. Three baseline operating modes. Infinite tuning. Expand autonomy as confidence builds. Pull it back whenever the risk profile changes.

Start with maximum oversight on day one. Agents propose, your team decides, and nothing moves without explicit approval. As the platform accumulates confidence across successive runs, oversight parameters expand in step with demonstrated performance.

Mature continuous operations can reach high autonomy, where engineers receive exception escalations and periodic digests rather than reviewing every commit. Every autonomy setting is configurable at the environment level, the policy level, and the individual change classification level. You pull it back whenever the risk profile demands it.

— 05 / Deployment Options

Every deployment model your regulated environment demands, supported without compromise.

The right deployment model is determined by your regulatory obligations, your internal risk policy, and the sensitivity of the systems Synthetix will operate on. For organisations where cloud is the answer, multi-tenant SaaS is available across US, EU, and APAC regions with data residency pinning. For those requiring dedicated infrastructure, single-tenant VPC deployment runs within your own AWS, Azure, or GCP account. For on-premises mandates, Synthetix deploys as a Kubernetes-native workload on your own infrastructure. For sovereign and classified environments, fully air-gapped operation is supported with updates delivered via signed and verified bundles.

— Default

SaaS

Multi-tenant cloud. Fastest to start. Available in US, EU, and APAC regions. Region pinning for data residency.

— Isolated

Single-tenant VPC

Dedicated VPC in your AWS, Azure, or GCP account. Customer-managed keys. No shared infrastructure.

— On-Prem

Self-hosted

Run Synthetix on your own infrastructure — on-premises, VMware, or OpenShift. Kubernetes-native deployment.

— Regulated

Air-gapped

Fully disconnected operation for sovereign, classified, and life-safety environments. Updates via signed and verified bundles.
